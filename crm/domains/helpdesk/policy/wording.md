# FastFix IT Support: Support Wording

Version 2026.1, for support plans bought or renewed on or after October 1, 2026.

This document explains what your FastFix support plan covers, what it leaves out and how we work out a service credit. Your plan record shows your plan, the sites it covers, the named contacts and the dates of your support term. All amounts are in US dollars.

## 1. Your support plan

| | Standard | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Response target | 8 business hours | 2 hours, around the clock |
| Per-incident cap | $150 | $400 |
| Priority multiplier | none | 1.5 |

Business hours are 8:00 to 18:00 in the time zone of your main site, Monday to Friday, without public holidays. A response target counts in hours from your ticket to the first reply from our engineer. The per-incident cap is the most we credit for one incident.

## 2. Service credits

A service credit is money back on your plan fee. We pay a credit when an outage of our platform caused your incident, or when we missed a response target. We do not pay credits as pay for lost profit.

## 3. Platform outages

We credit each hour of a platform outage, or part of an hour, at $60, up to your plan's per-incident cap. A platform outage is a period on our status page that names one of our services as down and your account among the accounts affected. The outage runs from the start time on the status page to the time it shows as resolved.

## 4. Response targets

We credit a flat $20 for each response target we missed. We miss a target when our first engineer's reply comes more than five minutes after the target. The reply must come from a person on our support staff and must name your ticket. The outage credit and the missed response credits for one incident together cannot go past your plan's per-incident cap.

## 5. How to open a ticket

Open your ticket within 30 days of the incident. Tell us what happened in your own words, list what you claim and attach the evidence in section 7. A ticket without its evidence waits until the evidence arrives.

## 6. What we do not credit

- A fault in your own equipment, network or settings.
- Planned maintenance, which we announce on the status page at least five days before it starts.
- A failure of a service we do not run, such as your internet link or another company's cloud service.
- An incident outside the dates of your support term.
- Indirect losses, which follow indirectly from an incident, such as lost sales, missed deadlines or contractual penalties.
- A ticket supported by false statements or altered documents.

## 7. Evidence

An outage claim needs our status page report for the incident. A missed response claim needs the ticket timeline. Add your own monitoring graphs when you have them. We read our status page and our ticket timeline over any other record, including your estimate.

## 8. The Priority multiplier

On the Priority plan we multiply the credit for an incident by 1.5. We apply the multiplier after we take off any credit already issued for the same incident and before we apply the per-incident cap. The Standard plan carries no multiplier.

## 9. Repeat incidents

We review a customer's third incident within any six month period before we decide it. A support lead reads the three incidents together and decides the third one. Count incidents by the date the ticket was filed, and count every ticket, credited, closed or open. A first or second incident in six months is decided in the normal way. The review exists to find a pattern that one agent cannot see, and most third incidents are paid or resolved after it.

## 10. How we work out your credit

1. We add the amount of each line on the ticket.
2. We take off any credit we have already issued for the same incident.
3. On the Priority plan we multiply the result by 1.5.
4. We cap the result at your plan's per-incident cap.
5. We pay the result as a credit on your plan fee, and nothing below zero.

## 11. Definitions

- **Covered sites:** the sites on your plan record.
- **Engineer:** a member of our support staff who replies on a ticket.
- **Incident:** one outage, or one missed response target, with the ticket that reports it.
- **Named contact:** a person on your plan record who may open tickets.
- **Per-incident cap:** the most we credit for one incident.
- **Service credit:** money back on your plan fee under this wording.
- **Status page:** our page at status.fastfix.example, which records outages and planned maintenance.
- **Support term:** the dates on your plan record, from the start date to the end date.
