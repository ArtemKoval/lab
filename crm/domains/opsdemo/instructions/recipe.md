# The recipe

1. Read the round: its objectives, its spending limit, and what the current version scored.
2. Read the version's graded escalations. Find where the reasons part from our workers' reasons.
3. Change one thing at a time: the instructions first, then the model, then the reasoning effort.
4. Try the change on the evaluation set. Keep what does better, and roll back what does not.
5. Stop when a change does not improve on the best version so far, or when the round's spending limit is reached.
