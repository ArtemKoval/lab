# The AutoFix repair wording

The wording is the contract between AutoFix and the vehicle owner. It says
what each agreement tier covers, what the agreement carries, and what the
authorization pays. The rules here always come first.

## 1. The agreement

One agreement covers one vehicle. The agreement names the tier, the covered
vehicle with its make and first registration, the service due date, the
desk that sold it, and the dates. An agreement whose end date is past is an
expired agreement, and AutoFix pays nothing under it.

## 2. The tiers and their fault classes

The standard tier covers the fault classes brakes, hvac, electrical,
engine_management, diagnostics, exhaust, tyres, and battery12v. The EV tier
covers battery, charging, ev_drivetrain, brakes, hvac, electrical,
diagnostics, and tyres. The fleet tier covers every fault class of the
standard tier, plus gearbox, clutch, and cooling.

A fault outside the tier's fault classes is not covered. AutoFix pays
nothing for it, whatever the estimate says.

## 3. The base excess

Every covered repair carries the tier's base excess. The standard tier
carries a base excess of 25 pounds. The EV tier and the fleet tier carry no
base excess. The authorization subtracts the base excess once per repair,
after the covered fault lines.

## 4. The per-repair cap

The authorization never passes the tier's per-repair cap. The standard tier
caps a repair at 500 pounds. The EV tier caps a repair at 400 pounds. The
fleet tier caps a repair at 900 pounds. The cap applies after the base
excess.

## 5. The certified EV gate

An EV vehicle goes only to a garage with the certified EV mark. A garage
without the mark never takes an EV job, whatever its brand match or its
free slots say. When the caller's city holds no garage with the certified
EV mark, the desk refers the job to the service manager.

## 6. The authorization and the sign-off

The authorization is the sum of the covered fault lines, less the base
excess, capped at the tier's per-repair cap. An authorization over 600
pounds never issues at the desk. It waits for a service manager's
sign-off.
