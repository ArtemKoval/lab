# Northgate Public Library: Loan Rules

Version 2026.2, for memberships opened or renewed on or after October 1, 2026. This document states what a membership allows at the desk, how long a loan runs, and when the desk declines or refers a request. The librarian manual says how the desk reads these rules.

## 1. Your membership

| | Standard | Research |
|---|---|---|
| Base loan term | 14 days | 14 days |
| Longest loan term | 14 days | 28 days |
| Renewals | none | to 28 days |

A membership covers the members named on the card. The standard tier serves everyday borrowing. The research tier serves long study projects and carries renewals.

## 2. The loan term

Every loan runs 14 days from the day the desk issues it. The due date is the 14th day. A research renewal runs longer, and section 4 states its cap. A loan term over 14 days needs the lead librarian's sign-off before the desk issues it.

## 3. Copies and availability

The library holds one or more copies of every title in its catalog, and the catalog records how many copies each title holds. One request borrows one copy. A checkout is possible only while one copy of the title is not on loan. When every copy of the title is on loan, the desk declines the request, and the borrower tries again when a copy comes back. A request that asks more copies than the catalog holds free is declined to the copies that exist, and a bulk request counts one loan per copy at the desk.

## 4. Renewals

A research membership renews a loan past the base term, up to 28 days in total from the first issue. A renewal past the tier cap is not granted, and the desk declines it. A standard membership carries no renewals, and the desk declines a standard request for a term past 14 days.

## 5. How many loans you may hold

A borrower holds at most 3 loans at the same time, on both tiers. A request from a borrower who already holds 3 loans is declined, until the borrower returns a title. A borrower with an overdue title on their card holds no new loan until the overdue title comes back.

## 6. Returns

A borrower returns a title at the desk, and the desk checks the copy in. The copy goes back on the shelf the same day, and it frees for the next borrower from that day.

## 7. Special collections

A title on the special collection shelves, or a title whose list price is $200 or more, does not leave the building. The desk refers every request for such a title to the lead librarian, who decides with the borrower in person.

## 8. Cards in bad standing

A card with damage to its record, or an identity the desk cannot verify, goes to the lead librarian. The desk refers the request and states what the lead needs to examine.
