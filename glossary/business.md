# Business Glossary

- Change: one unit of work that carries proposal, specs, design, ADR manifest, and tasks from intent to archive.
- Capability: a durable behavior with one spec under openspec/specs/.
- Intent: the reason a change exists, recorded first and kept visible through every artifact.
- Gate: a condition that must hold before the pipeline reaches the next step.
- Handover: an issue that carries observations from a finished run to later work.
